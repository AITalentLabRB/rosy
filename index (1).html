05 — How I think about AI hiringprinciples

A job title tells you what someone was called. A repository, a paper, a contribution history tells you what they can actually do — and that's where I start.

01
Understand the system before the search

I learn the architecture, roadmap and capability gap behind a role before I write a single search string. Calibration is the work — sourcing follows from it.

02
Signals over titles

For AI/ML, demonstrated work beats keywords. I evaluate repos, contribution history, research ties and adjacent skills to surface people that profile search never reaches.

03
Recruiting is an iterative system

When a search stalls, the funnel tells you why. I read sourcing, feedback, offer aging and time-to-fill data to find the root cause, then recalibrate with the hiring leader.

04
AI should create recruiter capacity

LLM workflows matter when they give recruiters time back for deeper sourcing, technical learning and real candidate conversations — so I build playbooks people actually use.
